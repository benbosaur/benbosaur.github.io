Why I had to leave education & teaching May 20, 2026

Teaching is one of the toughest jobs! Yes the holidays are nice, but when most of the holidays are spent planning and marking they become a false perk.

Teaching is unpredicatable, the staff shortages are rife, leading to the constant cover of lessons outside of skillset, but also changes to curriculums are constant as the government adopts unfathomed alternative directions.

But it wasn't all this. It was the rapid avialbility of AI tools. Imagine spending your weekend, developing materials to cement fundamentals and springboard the development of students only to constantly have AI slop handed in for assessments and a complete lack of engagement in fundamentals. This became so unrewarding I had to get out. Student skills were deprciating cohort after cohort and I do fear for the future. My message to learners is use AI as a learning tool and not a replacement for your own craft. This message I tried repeatedly to get across but unfortunately, I may have had a better reaction from talking to a brick wall....
